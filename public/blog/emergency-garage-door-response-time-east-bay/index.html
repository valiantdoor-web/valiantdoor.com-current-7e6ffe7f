Emergency Response and After-Hours Questions

Can you promise an exact emergency arrival time?

An arrival window must be confirmed with dispatch for your address and current conditions. An online service-area listing is not a live appointment confirmation.

What should I disclose before an after-hours visit?

Say whether the door is open, whether your property is unsecured and whether another entrance is available. Ask dispatch to confirm the current service charge and what requires separate repair approval.

Does an emergency visit include a finished repair?

Not automatically. Inspection may reveal damage or parts requirements that change the work needed. Ask what can be addressed during the visit and what remains outstanding.
