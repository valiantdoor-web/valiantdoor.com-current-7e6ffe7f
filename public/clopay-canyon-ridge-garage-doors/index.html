Direct answers

Canyon Ridge questions

What is the Canyon Ridge 5-Layer door made from?

Clopay describes the five layers as composite overlay, composite cladding, steel, two-inch Intellicore® polyurethane insulation, and a second steel layer.

What is the published R-value?

Clopay currently publishes an R-value of 20.4 for the Canyon Ridge Carriage House 5-Layer collection.

Can I design the door before requesting a quote?

Yes. Use Clopay EZDoor™ to explore compatible designs, finishes, windows, glass, and decorative hardware, then send your preferred configuration to Valiant for field verification and pricing.
