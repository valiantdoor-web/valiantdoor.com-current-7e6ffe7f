What Is A Wayne-Dalton TorqueMaster System?

Enclosed Spring System

Wayne-Dalton TorqueMaster places the spring inside a steel tube above the door. A conventional torsion setup uses exposed springs on a torsion bar. If a TorqueMaster spring fails, an inspection helps determine whether spring replacement or conversion is appropriate for the door, its condition, and the available compatible parts.

Why Convert To Standard Torsion?

Serviceable Parts

A standard torsion bar, springs, cable drums, and bearing plates use conventional hardware that qualified technicians can inspect and service.

Springs Matched To Your Door

We install DuraMaster powder-coated torsion springs from Service Spring Corp, selected and matched to the specific door. Correct spring sizing and balance are part of the conversion plan.

Conventional Service Hardware

Once converted, future spring replacements, cable repairs, and balance adjustments use conventional torsion-system components.

Before you book

Wayne-Dalton conversion questions.

What Is A Wayne-Dalton TorqueMaster System?

TorqueMaster is a Wayne-Dalton spring system with the spring enclosed in a steel tube above the door, rather than exposed on a conventional torsion bar. Spring replacement and conversion are different options; the appropriate choice depends on an inspection. Get a clear estimate →

Why Convert A TorqueMaster System To Standard Torsion?

A standard torsion conversion swaps the enclosed, proprietary spring tube for an open torsion bar, standard-size springs, and cable drums that any qualified technician can service with widely available parts. Request an appointment →

How Much Does A Wayne-Dalton Torsion Conversion Cost?

A single-spring TorqueMaster-to-standard-torsion conversion starts at $549, and a double-spring conversion starts at $649. Final pricing depends on door size, weight, and existing hardware condition. Get a clear estimate →

How Long Does A Torsion Conversion Take?

Most conversions are completed in a single visit, including removing the old TorqueMaster tube and verifying door balance and safety before the opener is reconnected. See same-day service options →

Will The Conversion Work With My Existing Opener?

In most cases, yes. The conversion changes the spring and cable system above the door, not the opener itself. We verify travel limits, force settings, and safety reversal after install. See opener diagnostics →

Do You Offer A Warranty On Wayne-Dalton Torsion Conversions?

Yes. Installed parts carry a lifetime warranty and new springs carry a 10-year warranty, backed by the Valiant Standard of Excellence. Get a clear estimate →

Do You Offer Emergency Service If The TorqueMaster Spring System Fails?

Yes. Call (925) 409-4974 for priority scheduling on urgent door safety issues. Avoid operating a stuck or unbalanced door until it has been inspected. Emergency repair service →

Is Clopay EZ-SET The Same As Wayne-Dalton TorqueMaster?

No. EZ-SET is a Clopay spring system, separate from Wayne-Dalton TorqueMaster. If you have a Clopay EZ-SET system, ask us to inspect the door and discuss its repair or conversion options. Request an inspection →
