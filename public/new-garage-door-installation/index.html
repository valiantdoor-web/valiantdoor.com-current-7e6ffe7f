What Valiant Checks Before a Door Is Ordered

  • Opening width, height, headroom, sideroom and backroom
  • Door construction, insulation and finished appearance
  • Track radius, lift type and structural attachment points
  • Spring engineering, door weight and cycle requirements

  • Removal, disposal, trim and project-specific corrections

Installation Questions

Before You Choose a New Door

What does a new garage door cost?

Final pricing depends on door size, construction, insulation, windows, finish, track configuration, spring system, opener, removal requirements, site corrections, freight and lead time. Valiant provides a project-specific proposal after verifying the opening and approved scope.

Can my existing opener be reused?

Sometimes. Compatibility depends on the new door's weight, balance, height, track geometry, opener type, condition, force and travel capability. Valiant verifies compatibility rather than assuming the existing opener is suitable.

Do you remove the old garage door?

Removal and disposal can be included when specified in the approved proposal. The final scope will state exactly what is included before work begins.
