The Blind Spot Above a Standard Photo Eye

A low beam can miss a high obstruction.

Conventional safety sensors are designed to monitor a narrow path close to the floor. A raised liftgate, protruding bumper, bike rack, cargo, pet, or person can occupy the door opening above that beam.

Homeowner Questions

Infinity Shield FAQ

What makes Infinity Shield different from standard garage door sensors?

Standard photo eyes monitor one low beam near the floor. Infinity Shield uses 25 infrared beams spanning from 3.5 to 71.5 inches above the floor, allowing the system to detect objects higher in the garage door opening, including an open vehicle hatch.

Will Infinity Shield work with my garage door opener?

The manufacturer lists compatibility with major opener brands including LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, Guardian, Marantec, Linear, and Overhead. Valiant verifies the opener model, wiring, track clearance, and bracket fit before installation.

Does Infinity Shield use the existing sensor wires?

Yes. Infinity Shield is powered through the garage door opener and is designed to connect to the existing low-voltage sensor wiring. Installation also requires the receiver DIP switches to be configured for the opener model.

Can Infinity Shield help prevent the door from hitting an open hatch?

Its full-opening beam array is designed to detect an open hatch or other obstruction that crosses the garage door path above the height of a conventional low-mounted sensor beam.

Does Infinity Shield include parking assistance?

Yes. The receiver includes a switchable beeper that can alert while a vehicle or open hatch is still obstructing the garage door path and stops when the obstruction clears.

What warranty applies to Infinity Shield?

The manufacturer states that each unit is backed by a two-year warranty and a 90-day guarantee. Manufacturer terms and registration requirements control product coverage; Valiant’s approved estimate or invoice controls installation scope.
