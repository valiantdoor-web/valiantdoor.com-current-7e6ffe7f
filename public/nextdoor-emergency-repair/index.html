Tell us whether the door is open, closed, crooked, or stuck partway, and whether you heard a loud bang or see a loose cable. Photos can help explain the issue before a visit. Keep people, pets, and vehicles clear of the opening, and stop using the opener when the door is damaged or unbalanced.
