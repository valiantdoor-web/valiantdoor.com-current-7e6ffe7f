Questions about the process

Do I approve the work before you start?

We explain the findings, scope and price so you can approve the proposed work before it is performed.

How do you document the work?

We use CompanyCam photos and notes to record relevant conditions and completed work, with the approved scope and job record managed in Housecall Pro.

Does a recommendation mean I need a new door?

We explain repair and replacement options based on the condition of your system. Future upgrades and maintenance recommendations are identified separately from work completed today.
